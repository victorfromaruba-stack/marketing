Why book direct

The difference is who else is on the boat.

Forty people on a party catamaran see the same reef you do. They just see it through everybody else's fins.

The morning, start to finish

Three hours, and it gets better the deeper you go.

Every trip runs the same shape. Shallow and bright to start, then out to the drop.

2m

Malmok flats

Straight off the beach into waist-deep turquoise. Fins on, mask checked, everybody breathing. Parrotfish and sergeant majors in the first two minutes.

6m

Boca Catalina

The bay where the green turtles come to feed on the seagrass. We cut the engine and drift. Most mornings you will be in the water with two or three of them.

12m

The Antilla

The largest shipwreck in the Caribbean, and shallow enough that parts of the hull still break the surface. Four hundred feet of it, full of light and fish.

0m

Back to the beach

Cold water, fresh fruit, and you are on the sand by ten with the whole day still ahead of you.
