## Priority order — currently a guess

Tier A: Tours, Guesthouses, Restaurants, Food Trucks
Tier B: Trades, Auto, Professional, Health
Tier C: Retail, Salon, Gym, Events

**This ordering is reasoning, not evidence.** It was derived from global no-website rates and
ROI logic, not from Aruba data. Scout should re-rank the moment the ledger disagrees — and it
may well disagree. Trades have the highest no-website rate anywhere and the best retention;
they could easily outperform the glamorous Tier A sectors.
