# Aruba Web Studio — Brand & Pricing

## Positioning

> **Top-tier craft at an entry-level price.**

We are not the cheap option. We are the *good* option that happens to cost less, because our
process is faster than an agency's — not because our work is thinner.

Never say "cheap," "budget," or "affordable" in any customer-facing material. Say **"honest
pricing"**, **"no agency overhead"**, **"you pay for the site, not the office."**

## Name & domains

- **Aruba Web Studio** — arubawebstudio.com (primary)
- `demo.arubawebstudio.com/<slug>` — demo hosting (Cloudflare Pages)
- `arubawebstudio.co` or similar — **separate cold-outreach sending domain**, never the main one
- Consider `.aw` later for local credibility (Afl. 89/yr + Afl. 30 setup via SETAR)

## Voice

Direct, local, unpretentious. Short sentences. No agency jargon — never "solutions",
"leverage", "digital transformation", "synergy". Write the way you'd talk standing in
someone's shop.

Good: *"Your menu is a photo on Facebook. Google can't read a photo."*
Bad: *"We deliver bespoke digital solutions that elevate your online presence."*

## Why these numbers (the reasoning, so you can defend them)

- **$650 vs the $500 you first considered.** The cheapest visible local competitor charges $750.
  At $650 you are still clearly the best value on the island, but you are not the bottom of the
  market. Being the absolute cheapest attracts the clients who negotiate hardest, complain most,
  and churn first. $650 also funds the demo-first process — you are doing real work *before* the
  sale, and that has to be paid for somewhere.
- **$35/month vs $20.** Afl. 63 versus Afl. 36 — the difference is invisible to the buyer and
  enormous to you. At 50 clients that is $1,750/month instead of $1,000. Over a year, $9,000.
  The buyer will not notice. You will.
- **Support allowance in writing.** One update a month, up to 30 minutes. Everything else is
  $45/hour. This single rule is what keeps the monthly plan profitable — without it, five
  demanding clients erase the margin from fifty quiet ones.

## Hard rules

1. **Never discount below $450.** Ever. Walk away instead.
2. **50% deposit before work starts.** Non-negotiable. It filters out time-wasters.
3. **Always show three tiers.** Never quote a single number.
4. **The monthly is not optional.** No site ships without a support plan attached — an
   abandoned site that goes down in year two damages your name on an island this size.
