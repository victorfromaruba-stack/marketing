House of Mosaic Art VillaArt villa and gallery, Oranjestad

A gallery that tells its own stories.

House of Mosaic Art Villa is a 1950 family home on Schotlandstraat that the artist Fernando Mansur has been covering in mosaics since 2004. The story behind each piece lived in his head, not on the wall.

Now a guest points a phone at an artwork and the piece starts talking. The camera recognises the artwork itself, so there is no QR code taped to the frame. Story Mode builds a collectible mosaic as they walk the house, and a VR gallery runs in the browser with nothing to install.

  • 13artworks recognised and narrated so far.
  • 5languages on the app’s screens: Papiamento, Dutch, Spanish, French and English.
  • 1codebase for the web app you can open today and the iOS and Android builds.

Hooiberg 165

Nobody pays us to find out.

  1. Tell us what the business does

    An email or a call. What you sell, and what you want somebody to be able to do on the site. That’s the whole brief. You don’t need to know what you want it to look like.

  2. We build one before you pay

    A working site on a real address, with your words and your photos. Not a mockup, and not a template with your logo dropped on it. You look at it on your own phone.

  3. You decide

    If it isn’t right, you owe nothing and we shake hands. If it is, half up front and half the day it goes live. We write the scope down first, including how many rounds of changes, so nobody is guessing by round four.

  4. It goes on your domain

    Your domain, your hosting account, your Google listing. One week for a website, longer for the bigger work, and we tell you which before you commit to anything.
