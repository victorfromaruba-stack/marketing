Selected work

House of Mosaic Art Villa

Boutique art hotel & gallery · Schotlandstraat 4, Oranjestad

A boutique art hotel with a gallery of original work, and a problem every gallery has: the story behind a piece lives in the owner's head, not on the wall.

We built the official app. A guest points their phone at an artwork and the piece tells its own story — narrated, in their language, triggered by the artwork itself rather than a QR code stuck to the frame. A guided Story Mode walks them through the collection and assembles a collectible mosaic as they go, and a VR gallery runs in the browser with nothing to install.

Fifteen artworks, each with its own recognition target and narration in four languages — English, Spanish, Dutch and Papiamento. One codebase produces the iOS app, the Android app and the website, so a change to a story is written once and appears in all three.
