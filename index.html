A gallery that tells its own stories.

House of Mosaic Art Villa is a 1950 family home on Schotlandstraat that the artist Fernando Mansur has been covering in mosaics since 2004. The story behind each piece lived in his head, not on the wall.

Now a guest points a phone at an artwork and the piece starts talking. The camera recognises the artwork itself, so there is no QR code taped to the frame. Story Mode builds a collectible mosaic as they walk the house, and a VR gallery runs in the browser with nothing to install.

  • 13artworks recognised and narrated so far.
  • 5languages on the app’s screens: Papiamento, Dutch, Spanish, French and English.
  • 1codebase for the web app you can open today and the iOS and Android builds.
